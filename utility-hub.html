❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Are my photos, text, or files uploaded to any external server?

No! Every single tool in Utility Hub operates 100% client-side in your web browser. Image compression, QR generation, text analysis, and AI background removal run locally on your device for absolute privacy.

Q: How does the in-browser image compressor work?

It renders the uploaded image onto an offscreen HTML5 canvas, resamples the pixel matrix using bicubic interpolation, and exports a quality-adjusted WebP or JPEG blob, shrinking file sizes by up to 90% in milliseconds.

Q: Can I extract Hindi or multilingual YouTube transcripts?

Yes! The YouTube Transcript module supports Unicode Devanagari character sets and generates beautifully formatted multi-page PDF documents with embedded Hindi and English typography.

Q: What formula does the Loan EMI Calculator use?

It uses the standard mathematical banking amortization formula: EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1], where P is Principal, r is periodic interest, and n is total monthly tenure.
