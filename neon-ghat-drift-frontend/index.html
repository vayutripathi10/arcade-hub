Neon Ghat Drift: Mountain Dash

Neon Ghat Drift: Mountain Dash is a high-adrenaline cyberpunk touge & mountain road drifting game inspired by winding Indian ghat hairpin roads. Slide around razor-sharp cliffside bends, grind inches away from glowing guardrails for Cliffhanger bonuses, weave past decorated Horn-OK-Please cyber-trucks, and unleash Overdrive Nitro!

🎮 Controls

  • One-Button Drift: Hold Spacebar, Left-Click, or Touch & Hold to kick the tail out around corners; release to straighten out for an Apex Boost!
  • Manual Steer: Use A / D or Left / Right Arrows anytime for precision lane positioning.
  • Nitro Overdrive: Press Shift, W / Up, or tap the NITRO bar when charged.

🏆 Trophy Milestones

  • Ghat Rookie: Complete your first hairpin drift
  • Cliffhanger: Spark along the outer cliff edge
  • Hairpin King: Clear 10 hairpins in one run
  • Horn OK Please!: Pull off 5 traffic near-misses
