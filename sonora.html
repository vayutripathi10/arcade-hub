Sonora Streaming Guide & FAQ

🎵 Sonora: Free Ad-Free YouTube Music Streaming with Background Playback

Sonora on Arcade Hub is an ad-free web music player engineered for seamless audio streaming. Listen to uninterrupted tracks, build custom playlists, and enjoy background playback on Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac with zero registration.

1. Ad-Free YouTube Music Streaming

Enjoy uninterrupted music without mid-roll commercials or banner interruptions. Paste any YouTube watch URL, Shorts link, or youtu.be shortlink into Sonora to start listening instantly.

2. Continuous Background & Screen-Off Playback

Unlike standard mobile browsers that kill audio when you switch tabs or lock your screen, Sonora integrates the HTML5 MediaSession API and silent audio bridges to maintain steady audio playback across tab changes and phone locks.

3. Curated Indian & Global Hit Libraries

Explore dedicated libraries including Karan Aujla Hits (Tauba Tauba, Softly, 52 Bars, Winning Speech), Honey Singh Jukebox, Satinder Sartaaj Sufi Classics, 90s Evergreen Bollywood, Road Trip Anthems, and Midnight Vibes.

4. Android Web Share Target PWA Support

When installed on Android via Chrome ("Add to Home Screen"), Sonora appears directly in the system share tray. Share any song from the YouTube app to Sonora to automatically add it to your queue and start playing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is Sonora completely free to use?

Yes! Sonora is 100% free with zero fees, zero recurring subscriptions, and zero email signups required.

Q: How do I export or backup my playlists?

Under the 'Saved' tab, click 'Backup' to download a JSON file containing all your saved tracks, custom playlists, and favorites. You can restore it anytime with the 'Restore' button.

Q: Can I use keyboard controls to pause or skip tracks?

Yes. Native lock screen controls, wireless Bluetooth headphone buttons, and hardware media keys (Play, Pause, Next, Previous) are fully integrated via the MediaSession API.
