Daily Quiz – AI Trivia & Knowledge Arena

Daily Quiz is a cutting-edge, real-time trivia game where artificial intelligence synthesizes 5 fresh, challenging questions directly on demand! Unlike traditional static quiz apps with stale question banks, every round is generated live in browser memory with zero database storage—ensuring infinite replayability, zero data footprint, and high-octane trivia thrills.

🎮 How to Play

  • Select Your Arena: Pick from 6 diverse fields: Cricket, Bollywood Cinema, World Football, Current Affairs & GK, World History, Tech & AI, or Surprise Mix!
  • Beat the 15-Second Timer: Each question gives you 15 seconds to evaluate the 4 options: [A], [B], [C], or [D]. Speed boosts your final bonus score!
  • Instant Feedback & AI Factoids: Discover immediately whether you scored a hit or missed the mark, accompanied by an AI-verified "Did You Know?" explanation fact.
  • Daily Streak Mode: Complete your daily sprint every day to build up your consecutive streak multiplier. Practice Arena is always open for unlimited training!

⚡ Game Highlights

  • Zero Database Footprint: Questions exist exclusively in client browser RAM during active play and disappear upon completion.
  • Dual Modes: Lock in your daily sprint once a day, or switch to Practice Arena for unlimited random 5-round blitzes.
  • Responsive Keyboard & Touch Controls: Press A, B, C, D or 1, 2, 3, 4 on desktop, or tap smoothly on mobile.
  • Web Audio FX: Procedurally synthesized mechanical clicks, ticking countdown clocks, victory chimes, and score fanfare.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How are questions generated without storing them in a database?
A: When you click a category, our engine queries Google Gemini AI with a strict JSON format template. The 5 questions are streamed into your browser memory, played, and discarded after review—leaving zero database footprint.
Q: Can I use my own Google AI Studio Gemini API Key?
A: Yes! Tap the ⚙️ settings icon on the top right and paste your free Google AI Studio key. It is saved in your local browser only. If you don't have a key, our built-in dynamic generator operates seamlessly with zero setup.
Q: How does the Daily Streak work without user accounts?
A: Your daily completion timestamp and streak count are stored safely in your browser's private local storage. No email, sign-up, or registration is required!
